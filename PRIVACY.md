# PeusicPlus Privacy Policy

PeusicPlus is an offline-first local music player.

## Data we collect

PeusicPlus does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data stored on your device

- Your music library metadata, such as titles, artists, albums, folders, and genres
- Playlists, favorites, recently played tracks, bookmarks, and saved queues
- Playback settings, audio effects, themes, and metadata overrides
- Cached album artwork references

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly export it.

## Permissions

- **Audio permission**: used only to find and play music stored on the device.
- **Notifications**: used for playback controls while music is playing in the background.
- **Photo picker**: used only when you choose custom album artwork.
- **Visualizer**: uses the microphone-related Android visualizer API only to capture the audio output waveform; PeusicPlus does not record or transmit microphone audio.

## Third parties

PeusicPlus has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data collection.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
